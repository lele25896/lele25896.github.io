About

PhD candidate in Theoretical Physics at the University of Catania (2023–present), researching mathematical models for complex systems and numerical simulations. Research intern at NCBJ Warsaw in 2025, resulting in an international scientific publication.

A physics background isn't a detour into machine learning — it's the training that makes the transition natural. Years spent building models, validating them against real data, and reasoning about nonlinear systems translate directly into feature engineering, rigorous model evaluation, and the ability to tackle problems never seen before.
